sealed-holdout and walk-forward validation, Monte Carlo risk, trial accounting and fail-closed execution,
with 2,700+ automated tests. Built with AI coding agents from written specifications. Both platforms
rejected every strategy tested, so neither goes live. Repositories are private; see the case study.
